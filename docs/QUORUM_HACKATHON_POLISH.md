## What changed

The proposed INR 1,499 monthly price is explicitly a founder's test price. The authored interview and test replay demonstrate how that objection can change the next product decision.

One Demo Autofill control still fills the active command. It now includes observation/source/excerpt/sample/date/limitations, a complete scripted interview and its processing checkbox, proposed experiment criteria, all existing experiment-result editors, Knowledge source metadata/text/permission, a concrete India-based legal profile, private task notes and the Live publication draft/confirmation. Normal primary actions remain separate. Fill itself makes no request, saves no record and publishes nothing. Undo restores the preceding draft, including checkbox values. Fresh forms retain their normal defaults.

The example's people, conversations and results are invented presentation material, not real customers. Saved observations, interviews and experiments carry a persistent Demo classification and visible labels. The server infers that classification from explicit `[DEMO]` markers too. Synthetic records are excluded from the real evidence decision and its counts. The Decision view separately shows a labelled, display-only rehearsal using the fictional records. A standalone adoption objection plus a missed threshold produces PIVOT in that rehearsal. Saving decision history still uses the real evidence rule.

Promoting a scripted interview finding retains its Demo classification. Live experiments with `[DEMO]` in their title also mark their ledger handoff as Demo. Their disclosure asks presenters to use synthetic `@example.invalid` addresses. Live visits and submissions are still actual captured interactions; no counts or contacts are invented by Autofill. A publish button click is required before any public link is created.

The Knowledge pack is authored fiction with an illustrative example.com URL; its limitations say that URL is not an external research source. Its location filter includes the imported India-tagged pack. Discover defaults to real Tavily search, with complete constraints. Switching to supplied-source mode reveals a real [Atlassian reference](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-management/scope-creep) and a short verified excerpt. The unknown publication date remains blank because it is optional; it does not require presentation-time typing. File upload/access credentials are not fabricated. A public experiment explicitly titled `[DEMO]` has its own single Autofill control for a synthetic `scopesignal-demo@example.invalid` contact and consent. Real visitor forms do not gain that control; the hidden bot trap stays empty. Filling never records a visit or submission.

## Artifact generator

`lib/prompts.ts` now specifies composition, typography, a substantive product visual, useful section structure, mobile behavior, truthful CTAs and proposed-price boundaries. `lib/artifact-design.ts` supplies a responsive static CSS foundation for those design primitives. The model still generates the idea-specific content, composition and mockup; no fixture response replaces inference.

This foundation styles anchor CTAs, hero typography, product cards, comparisons, mobile stacking, dark sections, focus states and reduced motion. It avoids making visual polish depend entirely on one model's CSS. The sanitizer preserves safe local section links, rejects remote/script links as before, strips active content, retains CSP and keeps the iframe's empty sandbox. Literal escaped layout newlines are normalized outside code/pre examples so they cannot become visible text nodes or extra CSS-grid children. Revalidation does not duplicate the injected design stylesheet.

Application literals, demo values, exported legal-reference separators and score placeholders no longer use em dashes. A central inference instruction and text normalizer remove them from generated copy/HTML, including HTML entities. Original source quotations, excerpts and imported notes remain verbatim for evidence integrity. Existing user-authored source records were not rewritten.
