# DEMO ONLY - fictional ScopeSignal pilot register

No recruitment, walkthrough, agreement, payment or customer test described here actually occurred. This register exists to demonstrate how Quorum stores and distinguishes observations. The numbers must be presented as simulated outcomes.

## Plan to enter before the simulated result

Audience: five owner-led Indian web-design agencies with two to ten staff and a recent client change request.

Method: approach five qualified owners directly, run the same brief/request/estimate/approval walkthrough, and ask for an explicit written agreement to a manually supported one-month pilot at INR 1,499 for one client project. Explain that the initial client portal requires an account. Do not count a compliment, email signup or unconfirmed intention as a signed pilot.

Pass criterion: at least two distinct agency owners sign the specified pilot agreement among at least five qualified owners approached. This is a founder-selected exploratory threshold, not a statistical or market-validation claim.

## Simulated totals and limitations

Five owners approached. Four demonstrated an inspectable change-request workflow. Two signed the specified pilot agreement. Zero payments were collected. Zero long-term usage or retention outcomes were measured. The pilot's small numerical criterion was met in this fictional register, while adoption objections remained unresolved.

The same fictional agencies appear in the interview and pilot examples. They are not additional independent participants. The register does not demonstrate representative demand, verified signatures, revenue, time savings or a successful product. A no-account approval link is a proposed next iteration, not a proven solution.
