# ScopeSignal - fictional founder brief for a Quorum demonstration

DEMO MATERIAL ONLY. This is an invented product scenario, not customer research, an operating business or evidence of demand. All prices, constraints and expected behavior below are founder hypotheses.

## Customer and problem hypothesis

ScopeSignal is intended for owner-led Indian web-design agencies with two to ten people and three to eight concurrent client projects. The proposed buyer is the agency owner or project manager. The founder suspects that requests arriving through email and WhatsApp are sometimes implemented before scope, price and approval are recorded together. How often this happens and whether it is costly enough to justify another tool are unknown.

## Proposed workflow

The agency copies the agreed project brief and a new client request into ScopeSignal. AI suggests whether the request may differ from the brief. A human reviews that suggestion, writes the proposed additional work and price, and requests client approval before work starts. The initial concept asks clients to create an account in a separate approval portal. Whether clients will tolerate that account requirement is a critical adoption risk. The tool does not determine legal rights or automatically decide what a client owes.

## Proposed commercial offer

The price hypothesis is INR 1,499 per agency per month, with a manually supported one-month pilot for one client project. There are no verified customers, payments, time savings or conversion rates. An expression of interest is not a purchase. Written pilot commitments, if collected in real research, must be distinguished from payment and repeated product use.

## Alternatives and constraints

Potential alternatives include email, WhatsApp, Google Docs, spreadsheets and the agency's existing project-management tool. These alternatives may already be sufficient. The founder can build with Next.js and has four weeks and INR 15,000 available for a prototype. The first version should handle one brief, one change request, a human-reviewed estimate and an approval record. No full project-management suite, invoicing engine or automated billing is included.

## Questions to investigate

1. Can owners show a recent change request and how they documented approval?
2. Is the problem missing software, or failing to use an existing process?
3. Will an agency owner ask a real client to use a separate account?
4. Will anyone commit to an INR 1,499 pilot rather than compliment the idea?
5. Would a client approval link without an account remove a meaningful obstacle?

## Evidence boundary

The companion interview notes and pilot register are fictional teaching examples. Their statements and numbers must remain labelled DEMO ONLY when entered into Quorum. They do not validate ScopeSignal or Quorum. Public professional guidance can inform the workflow, but does not establish demand among Indian agencies.
